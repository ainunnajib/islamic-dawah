# Dalil: Hisab Qat'i Can Reject Hilal Sighting Claims

## Core Ruling (Mazhab Syafi'i — Qawl Mu'tamad)

**If astronomical computation (hisab qat'i) proves it is scientifically impossible to see the hilal, then testimony (shahada) of sighting is REJECTED.**

The fiqh principle: **"al-zannu lā yu'āriḍu l-qaṭ'a"** — the probable (testimony) cannot contradict the definite (astronomical data).

---

## Primary Sources

### 1. Imam Taqiyuddin al-Subki (d. 756 H)
**Source:** *Fatawa al-Subki*, 1:226

> "If one or two witnessed sighting the crescent, whereas the judgement of computation is that it is impossible to sight the crescent — **this testimony is not accepted**, since astronomical computation (hisab) is **definite (qat'i)** while testimony and report are **probable (zann)**, and the probable cannot contradict the definite."

### 2. Imam Ibn Hajar al-Haitami (d. 974 H)
**Source:** *Tuhfat al-Muhtaj*, 4:508 (also referenced in *I'anat al-Talibin*, 2:216)

Ibn Hajar **qualified and explained** the conditions for hisab to override testimony:

> "If its specialists agreed that [for a given computation] its premises — its equations, axioms, mathematical procedure (purely rational premises), and also its **long-term empirical data and observations (arsad wa tajarib tawila)** relied upon to reach the result — are **definite (qat'i)**, and the reporters of that computation number in the **tawatur** [mass-transmission, too many to fabricate], **then the testimony is rejected**. If not, no."

### 3. Qadi al-Batawi
**Source:** *Sullam al-Nayyirayn*, 1:10

> "The judge must examine the testimony which must be in agreement with the conditions already described and with the **judgements of definite computation** as to the **possibility or impossibility** of sighting it."

---

## What Counts as Hisab Qat'i (Definitive Computation)?

According to Imam Ibn Hajar and later scholars:

1. **Moonset time** (ghurub al-hilal) — factual, not prediction
2. **Conjunction time** (ijtima' al-nayrayn) — when new moon is "born"  
3. **Moon altitude at sunset** — whether moon is above/below horizon
4. **Lag time** (makth/qaws al-makth) — how long moon stays above horizon after sunset

These are **matters of fact** (astronomical data), not astrological predictions. Modern astronomy calculates these with absolute precision.

---

## Application to Saudi Arabia's Ramadhan 1447H (Feb 2026) Claim

If Saudi claims to sight hilal on a date when:
- The moon has already set before sunset, OR
- Conjunction hasn't occurred yet, OR  
- The moon is below the horizon at sunset

Then per the **Qawl Mu'tamad of Mazhab Syafi'i**, this testimony is **invalid** because it contradicts hisab qat'i.

---

## Important Distinctions

- **Hisab qat'i** (definitive computation) = astronomical FACTS (moonset, conjunction) → CAN reject testimony
- **Hisab zanni** (probabilistic computation) = visibility predictions based on models → CANNOT alone reject testimony
- The scholars distinguished between **astronomy** (ilm al-falak) and **astrology** (ilm al-tanjim/ahkam al-nujum) — the former is legitimate science, the latter is forbidden

## Key Fiqh Principles Referenced

1. **al-zannu lā yu'āriḍu l-qaṭ'a** — The probable cannot contradict the definite
2. **al-nādiru ka l-ma'dūmi** — Something that rarely happens is like something non-existent
3. **taṣarrufu l-imāmi 'alā l-ra'iyyati manūṭun bi l-maṣlaḥati** — Authority's decisions depend on public interest
